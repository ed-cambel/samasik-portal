Borrowing Instructions

  1. Requests for borrowing should be done as soon as possible.
  2. Only requests 3 days before the event/activity will be entertained to allow the Tagapangalaga to prepare your items.
  3. The borrowed items are expected to be returned at the drop-off station on the date stated in the form (Expected Return Date). Residents will notify the Tagapangalaga when they have dropped off the items via Messenger (with picture).
  4. In cases where items are returned late, the Tagapangalaga may contact the borrower and gently remind them to return the borrowed item through the contact information they will provide in this form.
  5. Borrowers may notify the Tagapangalaga in instances where the expected return date cannot be met.
  6. Borrowers must ensure that all borrowed items are handled with care. In the event an item is returned damaged, the borrower is required to provide a replacement.
  7. Only requests related to Samahang Sikolohiya will be entertained.
  8. In cases of urgent requests, please contact the Tagapangalaga directly via Messenger.
