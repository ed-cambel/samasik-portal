ABOUT SAMASIK

The Samahang Sikolohiya (SamaSik) is a duly recognized organization at the University of the Philippines Visayas, dedicated to promoting mental health and Filipino Psychology through passion, service, leadership, and hard work.

Founded on June 24, 1993 by Hannah Grace Sonza

Organization

Objectives

  • To promote Filipino Psychology,
  • To foster responsible and harmonious relationships among students of Psychology, uniting them into one, active, and compact group,
  • To create an atmosphere for the exchange of ideas,
  • To forward the scholastic, academic, and intellectual interests of members, and
  • To extend services and assistance to parties/entities that will not only expose and contribute to the knowledge and experiences of members, but will likewise fill the needs of recipient parties/entities.

MISSION

A united community of Psychology students dedicated to service, and committed to promoting mental health and upholding social responsibility in building an inclusive, empowered, and mentally healthy society.

VISION

To strive for a community dedicated to advancing culturally relevant and empirically grounded psychological knowledge. Guided by the principles of Filipino Psychology, it strives to promote mental health awareness, foster unity and academic growth among its members, and extend meaningful service to the broader community.
